Ethics + e-commerce law = trust

Ethics and e-commerce law in online buying and selling, including electronic signatures and data security.

Shop and Sell Responsibly Online

Artist
Patricia Pepito
Medium
Digital poster, made in Canva
Subject
Ethics and e-commerce law in online buying and selling, including electronic signatures and data security.

Words of The Artist

500 to 600 words

The infographic entitled "Shop and Sell Responsibly Online" promotes ethical and responsible e-commerce behaviors. Since selling and buying products or services online is very common nowadays, people should understand not only how to make online transactions, but also protect themselves from fraud, privacy violations, and other online risks. The infographic composed of six tips of ethical e-commerce practices that is being explained and should be taken care of by consumers and sellers.

First, "Ethics + E-commerce = Trust" specifically highlights integrity through providing good quality services and products. This is supported by the Philippine law, Republic Act No. 11967 (Internet Transactions Act of 2023), which fosters protection for online consumers, sellers, and transactions involved. Next is the "Protect Personal Information", which obviously refers to keeping personal accounts, information, and transactions secured by protecting them from unauthorized access or disclosure. The third section, "Respect Electronic Transactions", motivates people to acknowledge online transactions as legitimate and should be addressed seriously. This idea is supported by Republic Act No. 8792 (Electronic Commerce Act of 2000), as it provides legal recognition to electronic data messages and documents.

For the fourth section, "Use Electronic Signatures Responsibly", this emphasizes the importance of using electronic signatures. Users must ensure that they understand certain agreements provided on digital documents before signing up and protect them from unauthorized use. On the other hand, the fifth section, "Keep Transactions Secure", suggest to use payment methods, strong passwords, and reliable platforms. Additionally, it is important to keep transaction records and documents for users since these can resolve disputes. Last but not least, "Protect Data with Encryption". This section points out the importance of securing information through encryption to prevent unauthorized acces from personal data such as passwords, personal details, transactional data, and payment information.

To conclude, the infographic wanted to discuss that responsible e-commerce is not only about buying and selling products. In this field, there should also be honesty, privacy, security, legal compliance, and respect for other people's dignity right. By following these practices and understanding Philippine e-commerce and data privacy laws, other consumers and sellers can help create a safer, more trustworthy, and more responsible digital marketplace.

The critics' corner

CA

Duke Charles "The Monocle" Ajie

Lord of the Rheagar Estates, appreciator of fine details.

E Commerce....

A good blend of the old and new laws citing RA 11967 (Internet Transactions Act of 2023) alongside RA 8792. Mentioning RA 11967 reflects an up-to-date understanding of contemporary digital consumer protection, merchant accountability, and trust mechanics in the Philippine online marketplace. It also directly addresses essential security concepts like Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability (the CIA Triad), Authenticity, and Non-Repudiation translating technical security criteria into practical e-commerce duties such as strong passwords, multi-factor authentication, encryption.

While it explicitly lists "Protect Personal Information" and emphasizes confidentiality and privacy, it omits naming the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), which is the foundational Philippine law governing customer records, breach liability, and personal information handling. Adding "RA 10173" alongside RA 8792 and RA 11967 would complete its statutory foundation.

Kristoff "The Chiaroscuro" Balatbat

The artist's use of the red-to-yellow spectrum of hues creates such a subtle yet intentional signal for observers to perceive the piece as of great importance. And with this fact in mind, as the flow of graphics leads your eyes from the text within each space to the ones below it, it allows observers to interpret the importance of the Republic's laws regarding e-commerce and what most can do to not only protect themselves, but also recognize what it means in the current digital age.
