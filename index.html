The Vanguards Museum

We stand at the front of all art preservation. Beauty first and foremost, forever.

Welcome

The Vanguards Museum is a small gallery built by six students for one course, COMETHICS. Each gallery room holds a poster that tackles one or more ethical subjects in the digital world, and every poster comes with a review from our critics.

What we stand for

Art made by people deserves to be kept and credited. We believe a poster, a painting or a doodle on scratch paper carries something a generator cannot copy: someone chose to make it.

What we preserve

Anything made by human hands and minds. If artificial intelligence generated it, it stays outside our walls.

  • MasterworksPaintings, sculpture and posters made with skill and care.
  • Everyday artSketches, digital experiments and school projects.
  • Scratch paper scribblesEven a child's drawing on the back of a page belongs here.
